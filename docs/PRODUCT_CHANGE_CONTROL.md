\# Product Change Control Rule



You are not authorized to expand the product merely because you identify a technically interesting possibility.



For every proposed change, classify it:



\- \*\*A\*\* — Required for current sprint

\- \*\*B\*\* — Required for product correctness

\- \*\*C\*\* — Required for grant demonstration

\- \*\*D\*\* — Valuable but post-grant

\- \*\*E\*\* — Outside current product scope



Only A, B, and explicitly authorized C work may be implemented.



For D and E:

\*\*DO NOT IMPLEMENT.\*\*



Instead add the item to:

`docs/POSTPONED\_BACKLOG.md`



Each postponed item must contain:

\- proposed feature

\- reason it is valuable

\- why it is currently deferred

\- dependencies

\- estimated complexity if known



If a requirement conflicts with the existing product scope, \*\*STOP and report the conflict before implementing.\*\*
